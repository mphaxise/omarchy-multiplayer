# Evaluation of slice 1: the review passes

Status: hands-on, 2026-09-02, 13:40 to 14:25 PDT. Build under review: the plugin at commit 3d5a06d, then 382c785 after the fixes this pass forced. Rig: the aarch64 UTM port of Omarchy quattro, Herdr 0.8.2, Claude Code 2.1.252, 1920x1200, software rendering. Every claim below cites a capture in `captures/screenshots/`, a line in the plugin or the core, a measured number, or a note from the morning's session; anything else is marked as interpretation.

## The result

Every mechanism behind the five success signals is live on the rig, and the two review passes from my design skill pack, `/ux-review` and `/design-qa`, found no P0 and four P1s, all on the panel's surface. Taking a fresh capture during the pass found a fifth: the keyboard cursor had never been painted, because my row component shadowed the shell's `hasCursor` property, so this morning's "cursor visible from open" fix changed nothing on screen. That defect, the clipped state label, the hero's "0M", and the caption text at 3.60:1 are fixed and verified in `sessions-panel-done-today-after-fixes_hands-on_arm-port_2026-09-02_live.jpg`. The other three P1s and the priority order below wait for my call at the keyboard.

Two rig facts came out of the same capture. The shell's hot reload after a plugin file change left the live widget on old code twice in a row; `omarchy-restart-shell` is what makes a QML change real on this build. And the five-second measurement for signal 1 is still unrun, so nothing here claims it passes.

## How I ran it

I gave Claude the two skill contracts, an evidence file with the measured facts, the nine captures from the morning, the plugin QML, the notification copy, and the specs the build follows, and had it run each skill as written: prioritized issues with fixes and open risks for `/ux-review`, severity-ranked findings with repro steps and impact for `/design-qa`. Contrast ratios are computed from the rig's theme tokens (Tokyo Night: background #1a1b26, foreground #a9b1d6, urgent #f7768e, accent #7aa2f7, muted #414868). Severity scale: P0 breaks an acceptance criterion or loses work or trust; P1 blocks or misleads the main flow, and WCAG AA failures count here; P2 friction with a workaround; P3 polish.

## Findings, ranked

1. **P1, fixed. The keyboard cursor was never painted.** `Session.qml` declared its own `hasCursor`, `foreground`, and `accent`, which shadowed the same properties on the shell's `CursorSurface`; the base component's `color: hasCursor ? fill : ...` read its own copy, which nothing ever set. `sessions-panel-done-today-before-fixes_…_live.jpg` shows the panel open with `selectedIndex` 0 and no highlight on any row; after the fix the shell log reports `hasCursor true fill #14a9b1d6` for row 0 and the after capture shows the fill and border. `current`, which was bound to the working state, is the shell's selected-item look (foreground at 0.18 plus a full-alpha border); dropped, so a busy agent no longer looks selected. Evidence: `Session.qml` lines 23 to 33 and 92 to 102; `/usr/share/omarchy/shell/Ui/CursorSurface.qml`.

2. **P1, text fixed, glyph and dots open. Caption text sat at 3.60:1 and the state dots at 1.91:1.** `dim` was `Qt.darker(foreground, 1.55)` = #6d728a, used for the state word, the agent kind, the meta line, and the empty and error rows, all at caption size, where AA asks 4.5:1. Now 1.3 = #8288a5, 4.89:1. The shell's own section headers and hero meta use 1.4 (4.28:1), so the plugin's captions are now a shade brighter than the shell's headers; that is the shell's call to make, and the plugin can only choose its own token. Still open: `muted` (#414868, 1.91:1) is the only color on the rest-state bar glyph and on every dot that is not blocked, so an orphaned row's dot matches an idle row's, and the bar glyph at rest is the darkest item in the bar (`signal1-panel-open-two-idle_…_live.jpg`, `sessions-panel-done-today-after-fixes_…_live_crop.png`). WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for a control's boundary and for a graphic the content depends on. Candidate: `alpha(foreground, 0.5)` at 3.03:1 or `Qt.darker(foreground, 1.5)` at 3.82:1, and a hollow ring for orphaned so the state holds without color. Evidence: `Panel.qml` line 40, `Session.qml` lines 38 and 94 to 95; `spec/03-sessions-panel.md` line 147 promises 4.5:1 for every state color.

3. **P1, open. The cursor can leave the viewport, with no sign that the list continues.** The panel caps at 640 px (`Panel.qml` line 485); six rows overflow it (`signal1-panel-open-two-idle_…_live.jpg` cuts the sixth row at y 655; the after capture cuts the sixth at the same place). `onMoveRequested` clamps the index and scrolls nothing (lines 491 to 501), the `ScrollBar.AsNeeded` handle is absent at rest in every capture, and Done today keeps 24 hours of stopped sessions, so this is the ordinary afternoon state. Enter and the two-press Stop act on the cursor row, so a person can open or stop a row they cannot see. Fix: a ListView, or `contentY` from the delegate's mapped `y`, and a visible scrollbar whenever `contentHeight > height`.

4. **P1, open. Open, Send, and Stop fire blind.** All three are `Quickshell.execDetached` with no result read back (`Panel.qml` lines 351 to 365); the CLI defines exit codes 3, 4, and 5 for exactly these failures (`spec/02-command-surface.md` lines 7 to 15). Send and Stop stay enabled on stopped rows (`Session.qml`; the after capture shows all three buttons under every stopped row), Receipt shows on live rows before a receipt exists, and Enter with Herdr down exits 4 in silence. One divergence from the spec to settle: the spec drops a `send` to a blocked agent with `instruction.dropped: agent_blocked`; the build delivers it through `agent.prompt` after an `agent.wait` and only records a drop when Herdr refuses (`omarchy-agent-session-core` lines 1764 to 1794). Whether a harness at a permission prompt accepts that text is untested. The stop spinner from this morning is the right shape; it clears itself after 30 s on a timer, which reads as success when the stop failed. Fix: run the three commands through a `Process`, print the exit code's meaning into the row's state slot (sent; queued, delivers on open; not delivered, agent is waiting on you; couldn't open, Herdr is not running), and gate the buttons on state.

5. **P2, open. The row and the toast identify an agent by name alone.** `list --json` carries id, name, kind, state, since, owner, branch, children, and `needs_attention`; goal and mode live only in `show --json` (`omarchy-agent-session-core` lines 1504 to 1513). The meta line is branch, owner, children, and on this rig every row reads `omarchy`, the login user (both panel captures). Keybinding launches are named `claude-<random>` (`claude-qf4rfj`, `claude-f6dqdn`), and the toast then reads "claude-qf4rfj needs approval". Two crash sessions share the name `crash-17157` because name uniqueness holds only among live sessions. Fix: goal and mode in `list --json`, the goal's first line as the row's second line, mode as a tag, and default names from the working directory and branch or the prompt's first words.

6. **P2, open. Waiting and blocked collapse into one state, and the copy over-claims.** Herdr reports a question and a permission prompt both as `blocked`; the row says `blocked`, the toast says `needs approval`, the section says Needs you. The spec's `waiting` color in the bar and the "needs an answer" toast are dead code today. Until the hook payload distinguishes the two, the honest copy is the same on every surface: `needs you`, with the body `claude · <goal> · Click to open and answer`. Evidence: `bin/omarchy-agent-session-watch` lines 60 to 140; `Panel.qml` lines 333 to 339.

7. **P2, open. Orphaned ranks last and counts toward nothing; the Herdr-down row is unbuilt.** Orphaned is concatenated last, so `maxRows` trims it first (`Panel.qml` line 328); it counts toward neither the hero, the badge, nor the glyph (lines 330 to 339), so after a reboot the bar sits muted and the hero reads "0 working / Nothing needs you" over a list of orphaned rows. Enter on one relaunches the harness, with `--resume` when a ref was captured and a fresh conversation when it was not, and the row says neither. The spec's "Herdr is not running" row (`spec/03-sessions-panel.md` line 143) does not exist; `list` never calls Herdr, so a dead server never trips the error row. Fix: orphaned counts as needing a person, its state text names the consequence of Enter, and the reconciler's exit 4 surfaces as the spec's row.

8. **P2, open. Notification persistence is inverted, and the done toast promises the receipt while its click opens the terminal.** Session toasts for blocked use normal urgency and time out; Omarchy's crash toast is critical and persists, so in `signal1-bar-urgent-badge_…_live.jpg` the badge says 1 and the only toast on screen is "Process crashed: bash" from 36 minutes earlier. The `done` body says "Click to open the receipt" while every click runs `omarchy-agent-session-open`. `omarchy-notification-send` accepts `-t <ms>` and `-r <id>`, and the watcher uses neither yet, so expiry and coalescing are one flag away. Fix: route the done click to the receipt pager or change the copy; give orphaned toasts a dismiss path or downgrade them at reboot, since clearing the corner today means reviving agents.

9. **P2, partly fixed. The hero mislabels and repeats.** "2 working" counted idle rows (`Panel.qml` lines 561 to 563); "needs-you / BLOCKED · 0M" restated the row beneath it in larger type, and "0M" came from a clock that was older than the record's `since`. The clock now refreshes with every snapshot and one formatter serves the hero and the rows ("just now", "3m", "1h 20m"). Still open: the spec's measurement hook says the first Needs-you row carries the larger type, and the build gives it to the hero. Proposed copy: calm, "Nothing needs you" over "2 idle · 3 done today"; one blocked, the name over "waiting on you · 3m · Enter opens".

10. **P2, open. The armed Stop outlives the cursor, and the cursor is positional.** `armedStopId` survives arrow moves (`Panel.qml` lines 491 to 514), `selectedIndex` is an integer into a list that re-sorts on every snapshot (lines 280 to 328), and the confirm label says nothing about children, which `stop` kills first without `--keep-children`. Fix: disarm on move, track selection by id, and render the label from the record ("x Confirm stop · 1 child").

11. **P3, fixed. The state label was clipped and the durations disagreed.** The name width subtracted the state label and 24 px, and forgot the dot and the kind label, so every row lost the end of its state text: "blocked · just" in `signal1-panel-blocked-session-leads_…_live.jpg`, "stopped ·" on all six rows of the before capture. The after capture shows "stopped · 47m", "1h 1m", "1h 24m" whole.

12. **P3, open. Motion, badge, targets, focus.** The stop spinner loops at 120 ms with no reduced-motion check, against the spec's own clause. The badge is 14 px with a 7 px digit and its top 2 px sit above the screen edge (`Panel.qml` lines 452 to 472). Buttons measure 58x28 px, fine for a pointer; the spec's floor `Style.space(4)` evaluates to 4 px on the shell's `Style.space(px)`, so it guarantees nothing. There is no Tab order; every action has a letter, so keyboard reach is complete. The panel shows no key legend.

13. **Rig, not counted. The locked-keyring prompt sits in every launch path.** Keybinding, Enter on an orphaned row, a toast click, and the crash toast all raise "Authentication required … Default Keyring … locked" (`baseline-a2-two-keybinding-agents-keyring-prompt_…jpg`) while the row reads `starting`. It is the image's fault; it lands in every recording, and the panel contradicts the desktop about who is waiting while it is up.

14. **Rig, verified. Hot reload leaves the live widget on old code.** After two `Local plugin changed, reloading` cycles, an IPC call answered from the old code; after `omarchy-restart-shell` it answered from the new. Which object survives the reload is interpretation. The rule is verified: restart the shell after a QML change on this build, and treat every "verified after hot reload" claim from this morning as unverified.

## Spec versus build

The build adds `new --prompt` (Herdr refused the multi-line crash prompt as argv) and a body line on toasts (the spec said title only); both are right and the specs need updating. The build lacks the Herdr-down row, the goal and mode on rows, the agent glyph on rows (a text kind label instead), and the `waiting` state anywhere it can render. The spec's accessibility clause claims 4.5:1 for every state color, a Tab order, and a `Style.space(4)` floor; the build met none of these before today and the floor is meaningless as written. The spec sends the `done` click to the receipt; the build sends it to the terminal. The spec drops a `send` to a blocked agent; the build delivers it.

## Decisions I owe this document

The skills end with questions for the designer, and I am the designer, so they are parked here for my next twenty minutes at the keyboard.

Which feeling is non-negotiable: the calm of opening the panel and believing "Nothing needs you"; the control of never having an agent act, stop, or revive without seeing the consequence first; or the safety of closing every window and knowing the work is still there. Findings 7 and 9 serve the first, 4 and 10 the second, 7 the third, and where they conflict (whether an orphaned session turns the bar urgent, for instance) one has to win.

Which option for the open findings. Option A, speed-first, one to two rig days (estimate): copy and bindings only. `needs you` on every surface; the hero copy above; goal and mode in `list --json` and on the row; Send and Stop hidden on ended rows; disarm Stop on move; a one-line key legend; a `toggle` and `openMostUrgent` keybinding; Done today capped at five rows. Leaves blind actions, scroll-into-view, the Herdr-down row, the waiting split, and naming for later. Option B, craft-first, four to six rig days (estimate): everything in A plus Process-backed actions with inline results, a ListView with scroll-into-view and a persistent scrollbar, two-line rows with actions on the cursor row only, orphaned counted and disclosed, the Herdr-down row, the waiting split when the hook payload allows it, default names from the prompt or directory, and a reduced-motion check.

Whether to approve the order above: 3, 4, then 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, then 2's glyph and dots, then 12. The alternative is 7 above 5, on the grounds that signal 2 is the promise I would least like to see break on a recording.

What severity blocks a public push. If P1 blocks, the slice is two fixes from clear (3 and 4). If only P0 blocks, it is clear today with a known-issues list.

Whether the residual risk is acceptable. Unfixed, a keyboard user can Enter or Stop a row they cannot see past six rows; a Send from the panel to a blocked agent has no verified outcome; after a reboot the panel says nothing needs me over orphaned rows and the only toast-clearing action revives agents; signal 1 is unmeasured and signal 4 is met by the sweep. The `/design-qa` pass recommends ship-with-known-risk for a public experiment repo on three conditions: findings 3, 4, and 7 verbatim in the README's known issues with their repro steps; no `live` capture published that still shows the 1.91:1 glyph as the only state cue without saying so; and no claim that signals 1 or 4 passed until the stopwatch runs and the notification path is attributed. The stop-ship case is that WCAG AA is my floor for anything carrying the Omarchy-UX method, in which case finding 2's glyph and dots land first.

The last question is whether I approve that call.
