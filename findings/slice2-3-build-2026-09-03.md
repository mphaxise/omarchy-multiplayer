## Built and unit-tested

162 tests pass locally (`python3 -m unittest discover -s tests`), up from 125. What they cover:

**Agent lanes (`spec/11-agent-lanes.md`).** `add` creates a lane as a child session with `spawn_reason: lane`, its own worktree on `session/<name>--<lane>` cut from the session branch, a pane split beside the session's pane in its Herdr workspace, the harness started in that pane, and the task delivered as the first instruction. Lane names default to the agent kind and stay unique. A lane's mode may only tighten. `lanes` lists main first. `send --lane` targets one lane; `send` without it fans out to main and every live lane, one record each. `done --lane --verdict kept` merges the lane's branch into the session's branch and records `lane.merged` with the commits; a conflict aborts the merge, blocks the lane with "merge conflict; open the lane to resolve", loses nothing, and exits 5. Every lane ending runs the completion path: `child.completed` on the parent and a marked instruction to its agent when live. `stop --keep-lanes` and `stop --lane`. The receipt lists lanes with merged commits; the loop view renders lane events. The watcher's toast reads "<session> · <lane> needs you".

**Watchers (pattern 06).** A parent watches every lane and child it spawns; `subscribe` adds any other watcher. Every non-instruction event on a watched session leaves one pending, marked notice on each watcher, coalesced (count and last summary) until delivered; the reconciler delivers between the watcher's turns and advances the cursor; `open` delivers whatever was queued while a session had no runtime, which slice 1's "delivered on revive" promised and never did. `log --since <seq>` is the `status --since` port. No log rotation: a 50,000-row log warns once.

**Closed-loop leftovers (`spec/09`).** `artifact-add --kind url` shows the link in the loop view and the receipt; `artifact-mark <label> --state live` relabels a capture in its sidecar with an `artifact.updated` event.

**Two people, in proxy form (`spec/12-two-people.md`).** A second identity from `OMARCHY_ACTOR` or the ssh client host; `visibility` private, draft, shared with `list` filtering and `--all`; an access list (`grant`, `--revoke`) with view, suggest, contribute, own enforced by the commands; `send --suggest` that waits for the owner, `accept` and `accept --dismiss` recorded by the owner with the instruction keeping its author; `assign` that moves responsibility and changes no access; presence in the runtime directory only, never the record. The watcher's body names an owner who is someone else.

**Panel (`Panel.qml`, `Session.qml`).** Lane lines on the cursor row, `l` walks them, `a` opens the add-an-agent field (`scripts/add-agent.sh`), Enter, `s`, `x`, and `r` act on the selected lane, a lane that needs you makes its session need you and names the lane; suggestions rank into Needs you with `y` accept and `d` dismiss and the suggester's name in the state slot; presence as "N here". Balanced and consistent by inspection only: the rig was off, so no `qmllint`, no restart, no capture.
